About Brew City Shows

Brew City Shows is an independent live music calendar built to help Milwaukee music fans discover concerts, club shows, and local gigs across every neighborhood in the city.

Have a show to add or a correction? Email us at hello@brewcityshows.com.

Privacy Policy

Brew City Shows respects your privacy. We do not sell personal information. Third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings.

External ticket links direct you to official venue or ticketing websites, which operate under their own privacy policies.
